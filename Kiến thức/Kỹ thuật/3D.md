# 3D in After Effects — Use when setting extrusion, bevel, camera blur, reflections or shadows: which renderer exposes which property, and the usual errors.

> Measured on AE 26.5 and rewritten for this MCP. Updated 2026-10-23.

## Three renderers (`comp.renderer` values)
| UI name | matchName | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Classic 3D | `ADBE Advanced 3d` | Default for new comps. The matchName is misleading: this is NOT Advanced 3D |
| Advanced 3D | `ADBE Calder` | The newer GPU renderer, with extrusion/bevel and coloured shadows |
| Cinema 4D | `ADBE Ernst` | Extrusion/bevel, reflections; slow |

## Properties gated by renderer
Not a hierarchy: each renderer unlocks a different set.

| Property (matchName) | Classic | Advanced | Cinema 4D |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADBE Camera Blur Level | yes | — | — |
| ADBE Iris Shape / Rotation / Roundness / Aspect Ratio / Diffraction Fringe / Highlight Gain / Threshold / Saturation | yes | — | — |
| ADBE Camera Depth of Field, Focus Distance, Aperture | yes | yes | — |
| ADBE Camera Focus Area Width | — | yes | — |
| ADBE Shadow Color | — | yes | — |
| ADBE Appears in Reflections, Reflection Coefficient | — | — | yes |
| ADBE Extrsn Depth, Bevel Styles, Bevel Direction, Bevel Depth, Hole Bevel Depth | — | yes | yes |

Setting a property the current renderer does not have fails as "hidden" or has no effect. `ae_do` adds a hint when this happens.

## How to work
- Check `comp.renderer` before setting extrusion, bevel, camera blur or reflections. Change the renderer only with the owner's agreement (it changes the look of the whole comp).
- 3D logo with depth: Advanced 3D, a shape/text layer with 3D on, `ADBE Extrsn Depth`.
- Particles on a logo (CC Scatterize / CC Particle Systems II): switch on Collapse Transformations or add Grow Bounds BEFORE the effect, otherwise the effect is clipped to the layer bounds (`template.logo_reveal` style particle already does it in the right order).
- Position always goes through `ADBE Transform Group`; 3D layers have a 3-dimensional Position, and `anim.apply` presets keep the Z axis.
